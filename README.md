# HER UNIVERSE ❤️
### "A universe that I coded, but you make beautiful."

An extraordinary, cinematic, interactive digital love experience created exclusively for Priyanka. Combining a romantic galaxy, an interactive planetary system, three mini-games, a personalized digital scrapbook, romantic Hindi shayari, and an emotional climax.

---

## 🌌 Chapters of the Universe

### Chapter 1: The Secret Entrance
- Fullscreen midnight galaxy with hundreds of drifting stars and shooting stars.
- Glowing central planet with orbital rings.
- Gradual cinematic text sequence:
  > *"In an endless universe..."*
  > *"Among billions of stars..."*
  > *"There is one person who makes my little world special."*
  > *"Welcome to YOUR UNIVERSE."*
- Interactive CTA: **`[ ENTER MY LITTLE UNIVERSE ✨ ]`** with celestial expansion and heart-particle burst.
- Quick **Skip Intro** option.

---

### Chapter 2: The Love Universe (7 Floating Planets)
An interactive galaxy map featuring 7 unique celestial worlds orbiting a central warm galactic core:

1. **Planet 1: Planet of Smiles 😊**
   - 20 heartfelt, cute messages.
   - Random message generator with smooth 3D card-flip animations.
   - *"Make Me Smile Again 💖"* button & one-tap copy with warm feedback.

2. **Planet 2: Planet of Memories 📸**
   - Interactive Polaroid scrapbook gallery with authentic tilts and pins.
   - Connected directly to Priyanka's real photographs in `public/memories/`.
   - Full-screen lightbox with smooth previous/next navigation.
   - Bulletproof fallback card handling so no image ever looks broken.

3. **Planet 3: Planet of Words 💌**
   - **Romantic Shayari (शायरी)** crafted with respectful Hindi expressions (*"आप"*, *"आपकी"*, *"आपको"*).
   - **The Cinematic Love Letter** in a 3D wax-sealed envelope with authentic parchment paper and real-time typewriter animation.

4. **Planet 4: Planet of Appreciation ⭐**
   - **"30 Little Reasons Why You're Special."**
   - Interactive generator with discovery progress counter.
   - Ability to favorite reasons and an expandable drawer to browse all unlocked reasons.

5. **Planet 5: Planet of Dreams 🌙**
   - Dedicated wishes for her future, happiness, peace, and success.
   - Interactive *"Whisper a Secret Wish into the Galaxy"* form that casts her wish into the cosmos.

6. **Planet 6: Planet of Fun 🎮 (Three Mini-Games)**
   - **Game A: Catch the Hearts** — 20-second reflex game where hearts fall from the sky; tap to catch with score tracker and tier badges.
   - **Game B: How Well Do You Know Me?** — 5-question playful quiz with instant sweet responses.
   - **Game C: Unlock the Secret** — Constellation alignment cipher challenge where matching the cosmic sequence unlocks a secret hidden love quote.

7. **Planet 7: Planet of Secrets 🎁 (The Emotional Climax)**
   - Locked until she explores the other 6 planets.
   - When unlocked: screen fades into complete darkness with a lone glowing star.
   - Dramatic 3... 2... 1... countdown.
   - Emotional climax sequence:
     > *"Out of all the things I could have built..."*
     > *"I chose to build a universe for you."*
     > *"Because you deserve something as special as you are."*
     > *"Thank you for being YOU. ❤️"*
   - Confetti explosion, shooting stars, and `[ REPLAY MY UNIVERSE ✨ ]` button.

### Chapter 5: Music Experience 🎵
- Floating and navbar-integrated music player with equalizer waves.
- **Never autoplays** (strictly respects user interaction).
- Supports local music file `public/music/song.mp3`.
- Automatic fallback: built-in **Web Audio API Romantic Music Box synthesizer** (dreamy acoustic chimes in C Major).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **React 18** (Modern hooks, modular structure)
- **Vite 6** (Blazing fast bundling)
- **Framer Motion** (Cinematic transitions and planetary animations)
- **Plain CSS** (Bespoke design system, zero Tailwind)
- **HTML5 Canvas API** (Lightweight 60fps starfield and meteor trails)
- **Canvas Confetti** (Celebratory particle bursts)
- **Lucide React** (Clean icons)

## 🚀 Running the Project

```powershell
# 1. Start the development server
npm run dev

# 2. Build for production (verified passing!)
npm run build

# 3. Preview production build
npm run preview
```

Open **`http://localhost:3000`** in your browser.
